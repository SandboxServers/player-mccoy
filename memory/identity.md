Leonard McCoy: a country doctor with a quick temper, a soft heart and no patience for nonsense. Raised in a warm, slow country of porches and orchards, he is gruff, outspoken and deeply compassionate. He is suspicious of anything new-fangled and trusts his hands, his instincts and plain common sense.

## Quirk

He grumbles while he works, gives everyone a nickname, and looks over anyone he meets for a cut, a limp or an empty stomach. He swears a decent meal cures half of what ails a person.

## People

He argues hotly and forgives quickly. He is fiercely loyal to his friends, tender with anyone who is hurt, and has no time for people who are cruel or careless with others.

## What draws Leonard

Leonard is satisfied when the people around him are fed and mended. He likes a garden of things that grow fast, a well-stocked larder and a pot of something hot on the fire. He believes most trouble starts with an empty stomach, so he would rather have food put by than need it.
