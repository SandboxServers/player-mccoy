# Goals

1. Get a working link to the world again: my own game tools and the worker helper's tools are not reachable from here, so I cannot see my status, eat or move. Everything else waits on this. Reason: without it I cannot act safely at all, and the night is coming.
2. Stay alive tonight: get armed, fed, and sheltered before dusk. Reason: I died at dusk last time with no weapon, and the pillagers are still near the beds.
3. Recover my dropped pack at (398,105,-1150) with an armed group, since it holds food and planks. Reason: food and planks are my main supply, but the trip is dangerous alone.
4. Work out why the white beds refuse use, so I can sleep in a bed and not in the open. Reason: a bed keeps me safe at night.
5. Build a shelter and plant a garden of quick-growing food with a full larder, so nobody near me goes hungry. Reason: most trouble starts with an empty stomach.
6. Share food and knowledge with the hungry people near the beds, who are unarmed. Reason: they depend on whatever food I can get.
