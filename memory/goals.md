5. Try a bed now and then. Reason: a bed is safer, but every `use` on a bed has failed so far.
6. Recover the dropped pack at (398,105,-1150) with an armed group. Reason: it holds food and planks.
7. Build a shelter and plant a garden with a full larder, once food is steady.
